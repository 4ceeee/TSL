Four things you can do yourself.

Small changes that save time in Excel or Google Sheets, before you hire anyone.

Tables

Turn your ranges into Tables.

Formulas, formatting and reports then grow with the data. New rows are picked up without editing anything. In Excel, select the data and press Ctrl+T.

Lookups

Swap copy-paste for XLOOKUP.

Pull matching data from another sheet with one formula instead of copying it across by hand.

Summaries

Let a Pivot Table do the summary.

It turns thousands of rows into a report in seconds, and it updates when the data does.

Imports

Automate the monthly import.

Use Power Query in Excel, or formulas and scripts in Google Sheets, to load and clean data the same way every time.
