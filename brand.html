Brand guide · v1

TSL, built one cell at a time.

The identity for Arc Dolorito's Excel and Google Sheets automation work. Everything starts from Arc's own logo sketch: the five spreadsheet operators over the letters TSL, drawn in cells.

01 · Logo

The primary lockup.

Operators in green, letters in ink. The operators read as a formula; the letters are the name.

02 · Construction

A 19 × 7 cell grid.

Kept exactly as Arc drew it, with one refinement: cells are square with a small gap and softened corners, so the mark reads as crafted, not pixelated.

Clear spaceKeep one cell row of space on every side.
Minimum size72 px wide on screen. Below that, use the mark.
CellsGap is 10% of a cell. Corners are 12%.

06 · The selected cell

One device for key numbers.

A thin green border with a brass fill handle, like the active cell in a sheet. Use it for the one number that matters on a page or post.

07 · Usage

Do and don't.

Do
  • Keep the operators and letters on their grid.
  • Use the reversed logo on ink and photos.
  • Let one number per layout carry the selected-cell frame.
Don't
  • Stretch, rotate or re-space the cells.
  • Recolor single cells or add gradients.
  • Set more than one serif phrase in a headline.
